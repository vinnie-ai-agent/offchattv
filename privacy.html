Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

OFFCHAT Publisher is a personal tool used only by the owner of the OFFCHAT accounts.

What data the app uses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. The app does not collect any data about other TikTok users or viewers.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); the local token is then deleted.
